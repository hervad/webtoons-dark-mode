# Privacy policy

This policy covers the Webtoons Dark Mode userscript and the Toonlight browser extension ("Toonlight: Dark Mode for WEBTOON"), which run the same code.

## In short

Toonlight collects no data. Nothing you do is recorded, and nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What is stored, and where

The theme saves its own settings **on your device only**, in your browser's extension storage (or your userscript manager's storage) and in the site's local storage for webtoons.com:

- whether dark mode is on;
- whether the reader dim is on;
- whether the edge shading is on;
- whether the reader's scroll-to-top button is hidden;
- whether the last webtoons.com page you opened showed you as logged in (a yes / no flag, used only to avoid a "LOG IN" button flashing while the page loads; no name, account or login details are read or stored).

These settings never leave your browser. Removing the extension (or the userscript) deletes the extension storage; clearing webtoons.com's site data deletes the rest.

## What is not collected

- No personal information, account details, passwords or payment information.
- No browsing history, reading history or page content.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry of any kind.

## Network requests

The extension makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code. It runs only on `www.webtoons.com` and `m.webtoons.com`, where it changes how the page looks. The one change to what a page loads: in the reader, the small ranking covers are requested in a sharper size from WEBTOON's own image server, the same server the page already uses.

## Permissions

- **storage:** to save the settings listed above.
- **Access to webtoons.com:** to apply the theme on WEBTOON pages, the only site the extension changes.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here, in the project's repository. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/hervad/webtoons-dark-mode/issues.
